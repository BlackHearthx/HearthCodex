# Localization

PT-BR and PT-PT are distinct locales, not aliases. Maintain separate complete tables in CodexText.cs. Localize phrasing, forms of address and terminology for each audience; do not merely change spelling or copy the Brazilian table into the European registration.

- PT-BR: buscar, registro, itens, chance; use the existing Brazilian form of address.
- PT-PT: procurar, registo, objetos, probabilidade (Prob. in the narrow column); use tu consistently (o teu, derrota-a).
- Keep identical wording where it is natural in both locales.
- Preserve format placeholders and markup, and check available UI space.
- Creature, biome, item and damage names continue to use the game's localized terms.
- Do not claim in-game visual validation based only on compilation.
